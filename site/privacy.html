Privacy Policy

Draft version 0.1, last updated 10 November 2026. Cygnix Labs (an LLC in formation; "Cygnix", "we") operates mcp.cygnixlabs.com and the Cygnix SEO MCP service (the "Service"). The final legal entity name, registered address and governing law will be inserted before this policy takes effect.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, plan, and the dates your account and keys were created and last used.
  • API keys: stored only as one-way SHA-256 hashes, plus a short prefix for display. We cannot recover a lost key; you rotate it instead.
  • Usage metering: counts of tool calls per key per month and short-lived per-minute counters, used for quotas, rate limits and billing.
  • Tool inputs and outputs: processed in memory to produce the result and not stored. The exception is white-label PDF reports you create on purpose, which are stored for 30 days so the private link works.
  • Google Search Console (optional, coming soon): when this feature is available and you connect it, we store an OAuth refresh token encrypted with AES-256-GCM, plus the granted scope (webmasters.readonly). Search Console data is fetched when you run a tool and not retained.
  • Branding (Agency): agency name, logo URL, colours and footer text you provide.
  • Billing: payments are handled by PayPal, Whop or Stripe. We receive a subscription ID, customer ID, plan and status. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Technical logs: our host (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses and request metadata for security and abuse prevention. We use IP addresses to rate-limit signups.
  • Public pages you ask us to check: the AEO and llms.txt tools fetch the public URLs you supply. They identify themselves as CygnixSEO-MCP/1.0.

2. How we use it

To provide and secure the Service, enforce plan limits, process subscriptions, send essential service and billing emails, and prevent abuse. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use your inputs, outputs or Search Console data to train AI models.

3. Google user data

Cygnix's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We request read-only Search Console access, use it only to provide the features you call, do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide the Service or as required by law, and do not let humans read it unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it. You can disconnect at any time with the gsc_disconnect tool or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.

4. Sharing

Only with processors who help us run the Service: Cloudflare (hosting and database), PayPal, Whop or Stripe (payments), and Google (only when you connect Search Console). We disclose data if the law requires it.

5. AI assistants you connect

When you use the Service through Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code or another client, that provider's own privacy policy covers what you send to it. Tool results we return are visible to that assistant.

6. Retention

Account and billing records: while your account is active and as required for tax and accounting. Usage counters: up to 24 months. Rate-limit windows: about 1 day. OAuth codes: 10 minutes. Access tokens: 24 hours. Refresh tokens: 90 days or until revoked. PDF reports: 30 days. Revoked keys: kept as hashes so they can't be reused.

7. Security

TLS everywhere, hashed keys, encrypted Google tokens, signed webhooks and least-privilege scopes. No system is perfectly secure. Report issues to info@cygnixlabs.com.

8. Your rights

You can request access, correction, export or deletion of your data, and object to processing, by emailing info@cygnixlabs.com from your account email. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have extra rights, including complaining to your data protection authority.

9. International transfers

The Service runs on Cloudflare's global network, so data may be processed outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and professionals and is not directed to anyone under 16.

11. Changes

We will post updates here and email account holders about material changes.
